About Me

I am a PhD student in Computer Science at Maseno University, Kenya, and an NLP researcher at the Maseno Centre for Applied Artificial Intelligence (MCAAI). My PhD research focuses on typologically-informed automatic speech recognition (ASR) modelling.

I hold a Google Africa PhD Fellowship in Natural Language Processing (2025–2028) and a DSA Visiting Researcher Fellowship at Sunbird AI. I am a co-founder and Head of Data at Tonative Africa, and a member of the Caribou Digital Pan-African Women PhD Scholars Collective in AI & Livelihoods. My work spans dataset curation, ASR fine-tuning, misinformation modelling, and community-centred data licensing for African languages.

Research Areas

  • Automatic Speech Recognition

    Typologically-informed ASR for morphologically rich Kenyan languages. Current focus: Dholuo, Kalenjin, and Maasai using LoRA fine-tuned self-supervised learning encoders.

    PhD Research

  • Natural Language Processing

    Misinformation classification in code-switched Swahili-English. Topic modelling, text classification and language modelling for low-resource African languages.

    MCAAI

  • Dataset Curation & Data Governance

    Led collection of 3,000+ hours of speech across five Kenyan languages (African Next Voices, Gates Foundation). Co-designed an Afro-centric community data licence under Mozilla Common Voice.

  • AI Policy & Digital Inclusion

    Research on leveraging language technologies for inclusive access to digital government services. Community-based focus groups on eGovernance accessibility.
